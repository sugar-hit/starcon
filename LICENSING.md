### Noncommercial exploration

The license permits use, modification, and distribution for noncommercial purposes, including qualifying personal study, experiments, research, testing, and hobby projects. When distributing the original, a modified version, or any part of the software, provide the license terms or their URL and preserve every `Required Notice:` line in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

Exploration is not an unconditional commercial trial grant. Evaluation, proofs of concept, or customer demonstrations with an anticipated commercial application should not be treated as personal noncommercial study; request appropriate evaluation or commercial rights first.

The standard license expressly permits use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations, and government institutions. It also preserves statutory fair-use rights. These provisions remain effective and are not overridden by this guide.

### Commercial authorization

Where the intended use is not already a permitted purpose under the standard terms or a statutory exception, obtain separate prior written authorization from the relevant rights holders for commercial activities such as:

- Running starcon for business operations, including product, order, inventory, or fulfillment management. Internal use without charging end users can still serve a commercial purpose.
- Operating commercial SaaS, hosting services, or products based on starcon.
- Selling the software or derivatives, or incorporating project code into commercial software.
- Providing commercial deployment, customization, or delivery to customers based on the project.

Charging a fee is not the only consideration. Public source access, downloading, forking, contributing, and attribution do not automatically grant rights beyond the standard license.

### Requesting authorization

Contact the maintainer through public contact details on [sugar-hit's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sugar-hit), or open a non-sensitive inquiry in [Issues](https://github.com/sugar-hit/starcon/issues). Describe the organization, intended purpose, internal or external use, planned modifications and distribution, and deployment scope. Do not publish credentials, customer information, or confidential business details.

Commercial rights require explicit written permission or an agreement issued by rights holders with authority to grant them. Fees, duration, deployment limits, modification and distribution rights, support, and liability are agreed separately. This repository promises neither automatic approval nor a default price. Where multiple contributors hold rights, authorization must cover the rights needed; maintainers cannot assume authority to relicense other contributors' work.

### Third-party materials

Third-party dependencies, tools, and images retain their own licenses and notices. The project license does not relicense them. Comply with their terms separately when using or distributing them.

For example, [`city-photos.ts`](packages/ui/src/lib/city-photos.ts) records photo sources, authors, and licenses, including Creative Commons terms. The code license does not imply rights to project names or logos, third-party platform names, or access to platform APIs.

### Contributions and ownership

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Contributions offered for inclusion should be explicitly made available under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, with confirmation that the contributor has the right to submit them. Identify third-party sources and licenses and retain their notices.

Contribution does not transfer copyright or automatically authorize commercial relicensing by maintainers. Obtain separate permission where such rights are needed. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Source availability and warranty

The commercial restriction makes starcon **source-available**, rather than open source under the OSI definition. Describe it accurately in competition entries and redistribution: source available for permitted noncommercial purposes; commercial use requires authorization unless already permitted by the governing terms.

The software is provided under the standard license's disclaimer. This guide adds no warranty of quality, production suitability, or support.

# 使用许可与商业授权

## 许可证

starcon 的项目原创代码及文档，除文件另有明确标注外，采用 **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**。完整英文条文见 [LICENSE](LICENSE)，官方来源见 [PolyForm Project](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0)。SPDX 标识：`PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`。

本页帮助理解项目的授权方式，不修改标准协议，也不扩大或缩小协议已经授予的权利。与本页说明有冲突时，以 LICENSE 和文件中的明确许可声明为准。

## 可以怎样体验

协议允许非商业目的的使用、修改与分发，包括符合条文条件的个人学习、实验、研究、测试和兴趣项目。分发原版、修改版或其中任何部分时，须提供协议副本或协议链接，并保留 [NOTICE](NOTICE) 中所有以 `Required Notice:` 开头的声明。

“体验”并非无条件的商业试用授权。为预期商业应用进行的评估、概念验证或客户演示，不应直接按个人非商业学习理解；请先向相关权利人申请相应的评估或商业许可。

标准协议对慈善组织、教育机构、公共研究组织、公共安全或卫生组织、环保组织和政府机构另有明确许可，且保留法律上的合理使用权。这些条款仍然有效，不能把本项目的商业授权说明解读为撤销这些权利。

## 哪些场景需要单独商业授权

在不属于标准协议已许可用途或法定例外的情况下，下列商业场景须先取得相关权利人的单独书面授权：

- 将 starcon 用于企业实际经营，例如管理商品、订单、库存或履约；即使仅内部自用、未向用户收费，也可能属于商业目的。
- 基于 starcon 运营收费或服务于商业目的的 SaaS、托管服务、商业产品。
- 将项目代码或衍生版本作为商业产品出售，或集成到商业软件中。
- 为客户提供基于项目的商业部署、定制开发或交付。

是否收费不是判断商业用途的唯一标准。公开源码、下载、fork、提交贡献或遵守署名要求，均不自动授予超出标准协议范围的商业权利。

## 如何申请

通过 [sugar-hit 的 GitHub 主页](https://github.com/sugar-hit)上的公开联系渠道联系维护者，或在[仓库 Issues](https://github.com/sugar-hit/starcon/issues)提出授权咨询。请描述使用主体、用途、内部使用或对外服务、是否修改与分发，以及计划部署范围；不要公开密码、客户数据或其他商业秘密。

只有具有相应授权权利的权利人签发的明确书面许可或协议，才能作为商业使用依据。授权费用、期限、部署数量、修改与分发权限、支持服务和责任范围由双方另行约定；本仓库不承诺自动授权，也不提供默认商业报价。涉及多个贡献者的代码时，商业授权应覆盖所需权利，维护者不能默认代表其他贡献者重新许可。

第三方依赖、工具和图片保留各自许可证及版权声明，不因项目根许可证而改为 PolyForm。使用与再分发时须分别履行这些条件。

例如，城市照片的来源、作者和许可记录在 [`city-photos.ts`](packages/ui/src/lib/city-photos.ts) 中；其中的 Creative Commons 素材按其原有协议使用。项目名称与标识的使用、第三方平台名称，以及平台 API 访问权限，也不能仅凭代码许可证推定取得。

## 贡献与权利归属

贡献者保留其原创贡献的版权。提交供本项目收录的贡献，应明确同意按本项目的 PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 条款提供该贡献，并确保有权提交。第三方代码或素材应注明来源及其许可，不得移除原有声明。

这不构成版权转让，也不自动允许维护者将贡献者的代码另行商业许可；如需此类权利，应另行取得贡献者的明确授权。具体提交流程见 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)。

## 源码公开与开源的区别

本项目允许查看源码和在协议范围内使用，但限制商业用途，因此属于 **source-available（源码可用）**。比赛、宣传和再分发时，应使用“源码公开，非商业许可；商业使用需授权”等准确表述，避免称为允许任意商用的开源软件。
